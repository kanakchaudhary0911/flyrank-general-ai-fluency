# Consistency, Not Talent (and Frame, Not Upstage)

This assignment brings together the portfolio planning, visual identity, and image curation decisions completed during Week 3 of the **FlyRank AI Fluency Program**.

The focus was on making deliberate choices that create a consistent portfolio system while ensuring that the design frames the work rather than competing with it.

---

## Week 3 Work Included

### 01 — The Through-Line: Map Content & CTAs

The portfolio content structure was defined through:

- One-line claim
- Page-by-page content map
- Case study placement
- Named calls to action
- Still-need-to-gather proof list

**Source:** `01_FL-07_The_Through-Line_Content_Map`

---

### 02 — Decide Once: Build Your Identity Kit

A simple and reusable visual identity was created using:

- **Heading Font:** Playfair Display
- **Body Font:** Inter
- **Palette:** Restrained lavender/purple with near-black and off-white
- **Logo:** KC monogram
- **Style Guide:** Reusable typography, color, spacing, and visual rules

**Source:** `02_FL-08_Decide_Once_Build_Your_Identity_Kit`

---

### 03 — Kill Your Darlings: Curate Your Images

The final image set focuses primarily on real captures of completed work.

Selected project visuals include:

- Advanced Calculator
- Portfolio-Kanak
- AI Internship Landing Page

Real screenshots were preferred over AI-generated stand-ins because they provide direct evidence of the actual work.

The rejected-image decision is also documented to show the reasoning behind the curation.

**Source:** `03_FL-09_Kill_Your_Darlings_Curate_Your_Images`

---

## Core Principle

> **The design is the frame, not the painting. The work is the painting.**

The visual system is intentionally calm and restrained so that projects, screenshots, case studies, and technical proof remain the primary focus.

---

## What I Practiced

- Content mapping
- CTA planning
- Visual identity design
- Typography selection
- Palette selection
- Style-system consistency
- Image curation
- Real-vs-AI visual judgment
- Rejection and selection decisions

---

## Final Outcome

The result is a portfolio direction where the content, visual identity, and imagery work as one system.

The goal is not to make the portfolio itself the most visually memorable element. The goal is to create a quiet, consistent frame that makes the actual work easier to understand and remember.
