## 1. Brand Direction

My portfolio identity is designed to feel:

> **Minimal, calm, modern, and professional.**

The visual identity uses a restrained lavender and purple palette with near-black text and an off-white background. The design is intentionally quiet so that projects, case studies, screenshots, and technical work remain the main focus.

---

## 2. Typography

### Heading Font

**Playfair Display**

Used for:
- Main portfolio headings
- Section titles
- Important visual statements

### Body Font

**Inter**

Used for:
- Body text
- Navigation
- Project descriptions
- Buttons
- Labels
- Supporting information

### Typography Rule

Use typography consistently across every page.

- Playfair Display → headings
- Inter → body and interface text
- Avoid adding decorative or unnecessary fonts

---

## 3. Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Lavender | Lavender Purple | `#8B6FC7` |
| Near-Black | Deep Charcoal | `#17151F` |
| Background | Soft Off-White | `#FAF8FC` |
| Accent | Soft Violet | `#B89AE8` |

### Palette Rule

The palette is intentionally limited to four colors.

The lavender/purple tones provide the visual identity, while near-black and off-white maintain readability and keep the portfolio professional.
