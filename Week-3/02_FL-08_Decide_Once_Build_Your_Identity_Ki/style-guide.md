## 1. Core Style

**Mood:** Minimal, calm, modern, and professional.

The portfolio should use a quiet visual system where the projects, case studies, screenshots, and technical proof remain the main focus.

---

## 2. Typography

### Headings — Playfair Display

Use for:

- Hero heading
- Page titles
- Section headings
- Major project titles

Recommended weights:

- Regular
- Medium
- SemiBold

### Body — Inter

Use for:

- Paragraphs
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Labels
- Project descriptions
- Supporting information

Recommended weights:

- Regular
- Medium
- SemiBold

### Rule

Do not introduce additional fonts unless there is a strong reason.
