# Kill Your Darlings — Curate Your Images

## Assignment Overview

This assignment focused on selecting and curating the visual assets for my professional portfolio.

The objective was to choose visuals that prove real work, support the portfolio content map, and communicate project evidence clearly without adding unnecessary decoration.

I prioritized real project screenshots over decorative or AI-generated visuals so that the selected images accurately represent the implemented work.

---

## Objectives

- Select visuals that provide real evidence of completed work.
- Choose images that support the portfolio content structure.
- Remove unnecessary or decorative visuals.
- Prefer real project screenshots over AI-generated substitutes.
- Keep the final image set focused, relevant, and easy to understand.
- Maintain consistency and clarity across portfolio visuals.

## Real Capture vs AI

I chose real screenshots for all project-specific visuals because these images need to prove the work itself.

AI-generated stand-ins could look polished but would not accurately demonstrate the implemented interface.

For actual project evidence, I preferred real captures over AI-generated images.

---

## Curation Principles

The final image selection follows three main principles:

### Evidence

Images should demonstrate real implemented work rather than simply decorate the portfolio.

### Relevance

Each screenshot should support the project or section where it is used.

### Clarity

Images should be legible and communicate useful information without unnecessary visual elements.
