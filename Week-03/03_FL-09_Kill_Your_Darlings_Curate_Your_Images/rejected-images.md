# Rejected Image / Generation Notes

## Rejected concept: generic AI-generated coding/technology hero artwork

I rejected the idea of using a generic AI-generated coding illustration as a primary project image. It could look attractive, but it would not prove that the project shown was actually built by me.

A real screenshot of the calculator, portfolio, or landing page communicates stronger evidence and avoids creating a misleading impression.

## Judgment
For project work, evidence is more valuable than decoration. Real captures were therefore selected as the keepers.
