# Draw the Path — Portfolio Sitemap + Toolkit

**Type:** Assignment
**Track:** General AI Fluency
**When:** Week 1
**Workload:** 3h

---

## Overview

This assignment focused on defining the structure and direction of my professional portfolio before beginning development.

The objective was to identify one clear technical claim, one specific hiring audience, and one primary action my portfolio should encourage.

Using AI-assisted discussions and portfolio planning exercises, I refined my portfolio strategy, created a visual sitemap, and established a structured foundation for future portfolio development.

---

## Objective

The main objective was to create a clear direction for my professional portfolio before moving into development.

This involved connecting the portfolio's technical positioning, target audience, primary action, and content structure.

---

## Task / Brief

The assignment involved:

- Defining one primary technical claim.
- Identifying one specific hiring audience.
- Choosing one primary portfolio action.
- Creating a clear portfolio sitemap.
- Validating the portfolio direction through AI-assisted discussions.

---

## Approach

I approached the assignment by first defining what my portfolio should communicate about my technical capabilities and who it should be designed for.

I then used AI-assisted discussions to pressure-test the portfolio direction and refine the structure. Based on this process, I created a visual sitemap and documented the resulting portfolio strategy.

---

## Work Done

### 1. Portfolio Direction

I defined a clear direction for my professional portfolio by focusing on one primary technical claim, one hiring audience, and one main action I wanted the portfolio to encourage.

This helped move the portfolio away from being a collection of projects and toward having a specific professional purpose.

### 2. Portfolio Sitemap

I created a visual sitemap to define the structure of the portfolio before development.

The sitemap provided a clear information architecture for organizing the portfolio's content and navigation.

### 3. Claude Interview and Pressure Test

I used Claude to discuss and pressure-test the portfolio direction.

The AI-assisted discussions helped me examine the strength and clarity of the portfolio idea and identify areas that could be improved.

### 4. Final Recommendations

I reviewed the AI recommendations and used them to validate and refine the portfolio strategy.

The final recommendations were documented as part of the assignment evidence.
