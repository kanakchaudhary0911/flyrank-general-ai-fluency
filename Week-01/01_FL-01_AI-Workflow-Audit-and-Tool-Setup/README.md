# FL-01 — AI Workflow Audit and Tool Setup

**Type:** Assignment
**Track:** General AI Fluency
**When:** Week 1
**Workload:** 4h

---

## Overview

This assignment focused on understanding my personal workflow and identifying how Artificial Intelligence can be integrated effectively into my daily academic and development tasks.

The goal was to evaluate recurring activities, determine the appropriate level of AI collaboration for each task, configure essential AI tools, and establish a structured workflow for future assignments.

---

## Objective

The main objective was to understand where AI can provide useful assistance in my existing workflow while keeping human judgment and responsibility in the process.

---

## Task / Brief

The assignment involved:

- Auditing recurring weekly tasks.
- Classifying tasks using AI collaboration strategies.
- Configuring essential AI tools and accounts.
- Creating a Claude Project with custom instructions.
- Defining measurable success criteria for future AI-assisted work.

---

## Approach

I approached the assignment by first examining my recurring academic and development activities and considering where AI could provide meaningful assistance.

I then worked on configuring the required AI tools and setting up a Claude Project with custom instructions. The workflow was designed to provide a structured foundation for future AI-assisted assignments rather than using AI without a defined process.

---

## Work Done

### 1. AI Workflow Audit

I reviewed recurring tasks in my academic and development workflow and considered how AI could be used effectively for those activities.

The audit helped identify tasks where AI collaboration could improve productivity while keeping the final judgment with me.

### 2. AI Collaboration Strategy

I classified tasks according to the level of AI assistance that would be appropriate.

The purpose was to distinguish between tasks where AI could assist significantly and tasks where independent work or human judgment should remain important.

### 3. AI Tool Setup

I configured the essential AI tools and accounts required for my workflow.

The tools used during this assignment included Claude AI, ChatGPT, and Anthropic Academy.

### 4. Claude Project Configuration

I created a Claude Project and configured it with custom instructions.

This established a more consistent environment for future AI-assisted work by giving the AI project-specific guidance.

### 5. Success Criteria

I defined measurable success criteria that can be used to evaluate future AI-assisted work.

This provides a basis for judging whether AI is actually improving my workflow rather than simply being used as an additional tool.
